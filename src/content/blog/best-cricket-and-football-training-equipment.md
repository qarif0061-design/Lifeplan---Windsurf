## FAQ

**Is an agility ladder useful for cricket?**
Yes. Quick feet help batters move to the ball, and fielders react faster. Use it 2-3 times a week.

**What age are these sets for?**
The listings describe them as suitable for kids, youth and adults. Children should train with adult supervision.

**Can I use a tennis ball instead of a training ball?**
For casual practice, yes. A Trubounce-style ball bounces more like a real cricket ball, so timing practice transfers better.
