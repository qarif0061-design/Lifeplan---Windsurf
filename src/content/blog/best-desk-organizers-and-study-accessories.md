## Set up a focused study desk in 15 minutes

1. Clear everything off the desk.
2. Raise your screen so the top is roughly at eye level.
3. Put only today's materials on the desk; everything else goes in drawers.
4. Keep your phone in another room during study blocks.
5. Write today's three study tasks in a planner such as our [Student Study Planner](https://faranh.gumroad.com/l/ctlzbx) or the [Daily Goal Journal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HPGW5QDP?tag=lifeplans06-20).

## FAQ

**Is a monitor riser worth it for students?**
If you study on a laptop or monitor for several hours a day, raising the screen helps you sit more upright. It is one of the cheapest ergonomic upgrades.

**Can I use a monitor riser with a laptop?**
Yes, but for the best posture add an external keyboard and mouse so your arms stay low.

**What is the minimum I need for a good study desk?**
Good light, a clear surface, a place for supplies and a written plan for the session.
