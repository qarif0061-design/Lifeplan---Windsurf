## 1. Xiaomi Smart Band 9: best overall

The [Xiaomi Smart Band 9](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D98DMX2H?tag=lifeplans06-20) has a bright 1.62-inch AMOLED screen, an aluminum frame, over 150 workout modes and, per Xiaomi, up to 21 days of battery with regular use. It is water resistant to 50 m.

- **Pros:** premium build, long battery, good app (Mi Fitness), reliable brand.
- **Cons:** no built-in GPS (uses your phone); price sometimes goes above $50.

## 2. Blackview R50 Pro: best for calls and big screen

The [Blackview tracker](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H2B42CR1?tag=lifeplans06-20) is closer to a smartwatch, with a 1.85-inch screen and the ability to answer calls over Bluetooth. It is an Amazon's Choice item with 1,700+ ratings.

- **Pros:** big screen, calls, two straps included, very low price.
- **Cons:** health readings are less accurate than premium brands; bulkier than a band.

## 3. ZURURU H98: best simple tracker

The [ZURURU H98](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4FKW8HC?tag=lifeplans06-20) covers steps, heart rate, SpO2, sleep stages and call/message alerts. It has over 10,000 ratings.

- **Pros:** cheap, light, easy to use.
- **Cons:** average rating around 3.9 stars; app is basic.

## 4. Amzhero Health Fitness Tracker: best slim design

The [Amzhero tracker](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CDR4KQRR?tag=lifeplans06-20) is slim with continuous heart rate and blood oxygen tracking and a 5ATM water rating.

- **Pros:** slim, comfortable, both band sizes in the box.
- **Cons:** no GPS; blood pressure readings are not medical grade.

## What budget trackers can't do

- Blood pressure and SpO2 readings on cheap trackers are estimates, not medical measurements.
- Most have no built-in GPS, so carry your phone on runs for accurate distance.
- Calorie numbers are rough guides.

## FAQ

**Are fitness trackers under $50 accurate?**
They are accurate enough for step counts and general trends. Heart rate during intense exercise, SpO2 and blood pressure are less reliable than premium devices.

**Do these work with iPhone and Android?**
Yes, all four work with both through their companion apps.

**Which is the best fitness tracker under $50?**
The Xiaomi Smart Band 9 when it is on sale; otherwise the ZURURU H98 for basic tracking or the Blackview for a bigger screen and calls.
